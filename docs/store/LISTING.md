## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

Lost something on your phone? Ask your pal.

FindPal is one search bar for everything on your Android phone. Type two or three letters and the right result is already under your thumb: an app, a person, a file, a setting, an OTP, even a quick sum. Press Enter and it's done.

🔎 ONE SEARCH FOR EVERYTHING
• Apps, including work-profile apps
• Contacts by name, nickname, email or part of a number
• Photos, videos, music, PDFs and documents, plus folders
• 60+ system settings: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, hotspot, battery and more
• Calendar events and recent notifications

👍 BUILT FOR ONE HAND
The search bar sits right above your keyboard and results grow up from it, so the best match is always closest to your thumb. Filter chips show how many contacts, apps and files matched. Enter shows what it will do before you press it.

⚡ DO THINGS, NOT JUST FIND THEM
• Call, SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram or Signal any contact in one tap
• Copy your latest OTP straight from search
• "timer 10 min", "alarm 6:30" open the Clock app ready to go
• Calculator, unit converter ("5 ft in cm") and currency converter ("100 usd in inr")
• Share files, open folders, jump to any setting

🇮🇳 MADE FOR INDIA
• Type "rahul" and find "राहुल": names in Hindi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi and Odia match English typing
• "payment" or "upi" finds PhonePe, GPay, Paytm and BHIM; "taxi" finds Uber, Ola and Rapido
• OTP chip for bank and UPI codes

🧠 SMART, AND IT LEARNS YOU
• Typo-tolerant: "whatsap" still finds WhatsApp
• Search by meaning with a small on-device AI model
• Learns what you open most and ranks it first
• Give anything a nickname: type "ma" to call Mom
• Shortcuts: "f report" searches files, "yt lofi" searches YouTube

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything is searched on your phone. Contacts, files, notifications and history never leave it. No ads, no tracking. The only download is public exchange rates for currency conversion, and you can turn it off. Every permission is optional, and you can delete all data from Settings at any time.

⭐ FREE AND PRO
FindPal Free searches your contacts: find anyone in two letters, in English or Indian scripts, and call them in one tap.
FindPal Pro unlocks everything else: apps, files, settings, calendar and notifications, OTP copy, quick answers, search by meaning, pins and nicknames, plus WhatsApp, SMS, Telegram and Signal from search. Try Pro free for 7 days, then pay monthly or yearly. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

📲 OPEN IT FROM ANYWHERE
Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile, the assistant gesture (long-press Home or swipe from the corner), app shortcuts, or an optional notification.

FindPal works on Android 8.0 and newer. Contact search needs only the contacts permission; every other permission is optional.
