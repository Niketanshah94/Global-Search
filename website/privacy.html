FindPal

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

The short version: FindPal searches your phone on your phone. Your contacts, files, calendar, notifications and search history are never uploaded to us or anyone else. FindPal has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't need an account.

This policy explains what the FindPal Android app (“FindPal”, “the app”, “we”) can access, what it does with that data, and what choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at SUPPORT_EMAIL.

What FindPal can access, and why

Every permission is optional. FindPal asks for each one only when you turn on the feature that needs it. Apps, settings and the calculator work with no permissions at all.

DataUsed forLeaves your phone?

Contacts (READ_CONTACTS)Finding people by name, nickname or number, and calling or messaging them when you tapNo
Files and media (photos, videos, audio, and “All files access” if you allow it)Building a search index of file and folder names. File contents are never read.No

What you type and openRanking the results you use most firstNo

The search index and history are stored in the app's private storage on your phone and are excluded from device backups.

Network use

  • Currency rates. When you convert currency, FindPal downloads public exchange rates (European Central Bank data via frankfurter.app). That request doesn't include anything you typed or any personal data. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Web search. If you choose “Search the web”, your query opens in your browser or search app, under that service's own privacy policy.
  • Google Play. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play Billing (see below).

FindPal sends no other network requests. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

FindPal Pro subscriptions

FindPal Pro is sold as a subscription through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that your subscription is active, and stores that on your phone. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone, because we never receive it.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can turn off any source (contacts, files, calendar, notifications) or revoke any permission at any time in FindPal Settings or Android Settings.
  • FindPal Settings → Privacy lets you clear your search history and the file index.
  • Uninstalling FindPal deletes everything it stored on your phone.

Children

FindPal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. In any case, none of the data FindPal handles leaves the device.

Security

Your data stays in FindPal's private app storage, which Android protects from other apps. Purchases can be verified using Google Play's signature, so they can't be faked.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
